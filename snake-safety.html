Give Snakes Space

Snakes are interesting to look at, but a wild snake should still be given plenty of space. Trying to touch, catch, or move a snake you cannot identify can put both you and the snake in danger.

Basic Safety

  • Do not try to pick up a wild snake.
  • Watch where you step when walking through tall grass or rocky areas.
  • Do not put your hands somewhere you cannot clearly see.
  • Give a snake room to move away instead of approaching it.
  • If a snake bites you, seek medical help rather than trying to treat the bite yourself.

Why Snakes Matter

Snakes are also an important part of their ecosystems. Many species help control populations of animals such as rodents. Learning how to safely live around snakes is better than assuming every snake is something that needs to be killed.
