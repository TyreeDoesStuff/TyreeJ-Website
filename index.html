Why Snakes?

Snakes are some of the most interesting animals to me because there are so many different types. Some rely on venom while others use their size and strength. This website goes over three snakes that stand out to me the most.

Black Mamba

Black mambas are known for their speed and extremely dangerous venom. Despite the name, the snake itself is usually gray or brown instead of black.

Learn About Black Mambas

Pythons

Pythons are powerful constrictors. Instead of using venom, they grab their prey and use their bodies to constrict it.

Learn About Pythons

Anacondas

Anacondas are massive snakes that spend a lot of their time around water. The green anaconda is especially known for its huge size.
