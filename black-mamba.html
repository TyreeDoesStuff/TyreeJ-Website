About the Black Mamba

The black mamba is a venomous snake found in parts of sub-Saharan Africa. Something I found interesting is that its name can be a little misleading. Black mambas are normally gray, brown, or olive. The black part of their name comes from the dark color inside of their mouth.

Speed and Venom

Black mambas have a reputation for being extremely fast. They also have powerful neurotoxic venom, which makes a bite a serious medical emergency. Their speed does not mean they spend their time chasing people though. Like many snakes, avoiding the animal and giving it space is the safest thing to do.

Quick Facts

  • They are native to sub-Saharan Africa.
  • They are venomous.
  • The inside of their mouth is dark, which is where their name comes from.
  • They can grow to several meters in length.
