About Anacondas

Anacondas are large constricting snakes from South America. The green anaconda is probably the most famous because of its massive body. They are strongly associated with rivers, swamps, and other wet environments.

Built for the Water

One thing that separates anacondas from a lot of other snakes is how comfortable they are in water. Their eyes and nostrils are positioned high on their head, helping them see and breathe while much of their body stays underwater.

Quick Facts

  • Anacondas are found in South America.
  • They are nonvenomous constrictors.
  • Green anacondas are extremely heavy-bodied snakes.
  • They spend a lot of time in or near water.
